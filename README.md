🌍 Shendam Connect

Digital Tourism, Hotel & Business Directory Platform for Shendam LGA

Shendam Connect is a modern digital platform designed to promote tourism, hotels, restaurants, businesses, attractions, and local services across Shendam Local Government Area, Plateau State, Nigeria.

The platform makes it easier for visitors and residents to discover local businesses, explore attractions, contact service providers, get directions, and access hotel information from one place.

🎯 Project Goal

The goal of Shendam Connect is to help make Shendam more digitally visible by creating a centralized platform where people can easily discover:

- 🏨 Hotels & accommodation
- 🍽️ Restaurants & food businesses
- 🏞️ Tourist attractions
- 🏪 Local businesses
- 🛍️ Shops & services
- 📍 Locations and directions
- 📞 Business contact information

---

✨ Key Features

🏨 Hotel Directory

Users can discover hotels and accommodation options available in Shendam.

🏢 Business Directory

Businesses and local service providers can be displayed with relevant information.

🏞️ Tourism & Attractions

The platform helps visitors discover places of interest within Shendam LGA.

📞 One-Click Contact

Users can quickly contact businesses through available phone and WhatsApp options.

📍 Google Maps Directions

Users can access location directions to listed businesses and attractions.

🖼️ Image Galleries

Businesses and attractions can showcase images to help users understand what they offer.

📱 Mobile-Friendly Design

The platform is designed to work across smartphones, tablets, and desktop devices.

⚡ Progressive Web App

Shendam Connect is designed as a modern web application with PWA capabilities.

🌐 Public Access

Anyone with the application link can access and test the platform.
